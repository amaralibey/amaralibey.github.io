I am a dedicated computer scientist passionate about **Computer Vision** for **Robotics**. During my Ph.D. at Laval University, under the supervision of  [Brahim Chaib-draa](https://iid.ulaval.ca/en/members/brahim-chaib-draa/) and [Philippe Giguère](https://iid.ulaval.ca/equipes/philippe-giguere/), I focused on developing advanced neural network architectures for Visual Place Recognition and collecting large-scale datasets to train them.

Prior to my doctoral studies, I majored in Software Engineering, where I developed a solid foundation in Problem Solving, Algorithms, and Combinatorial Optimization.
